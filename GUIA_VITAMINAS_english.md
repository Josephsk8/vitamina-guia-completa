## Vitamin Table
📋

| Vitamin | Also known as | Main benefits for the body | Common food sources | 🥗 Best combinations to maximize absorption | ⚠️ What to avoid |
|----------|----------------|----------------------------|---------------------|--------------------------------------------|------------------|
| **A** | Retinol, beta-carotene | Supports night vision, healthy skin, mucous membranes, teeth, bones; strengthens the immune system | Liver, carrots, sweet potatoes, spinach, chard, squash, cantaloupe, eggs, milk | **+ Healthy fats** (olive oil, avocado, nuts): increases absorption 2–3x (fat-soluble) | Avoid eating it alone without fat in the meal |
| **B1** | Thiamine | Converts food into energy; supports the nervous system | Whole grains, pork, legumes, seeds, nuts, eggs | **+ Proteins**: helps amino acid metabolism | Too much coffee/tea can reduce absorption |
| **B2** | Riboflavin | Growth, red blood cells, releases energy, healthy skin and eyes | Milk, yogurt, cheese, eggs, lean meats, green vegetables, fortified cereals | With foods rich in **protein** | Sunlight destroys B2 in milk (store in the dark) |
| **B3** | Niacin | Energy, nerves, brain, digestion, healthy skin, improves cholesterol | Meat, fish, peanuts, whole grains, fortified cereals, mushrooms | **+ Tryptophan** (chicken, turkey, eggs): body converts it into B3 | Alcohol reduces B3 levels |
| **B5** | Pantothenic acid | Metabolism, hormones, cholesterol, red blood cells | Chicken, beef, potatoes, tomatoes, broccoli, eggs, oats, avocados | **+ Complex carbohydrates**: optimizes energy production | Overcooking destroys B5 |
| **B6** | Pyridoxine | Red blood cells, brain, nervous system, protein metabolism | Chickpeas, tuna, salmon, chicken, potatoes, bananas, fortified cereals, pistachios | **+ Proteins**: you need more B6 when you eat more protein | Alcohol reduces absorption |
| **B7** | Biotin | Metabolism of carbs/fats/proteins, healthy hair, skin, and nails | Egg yolk, liver, salmon, pork, almonds, peanuts, sweet potatoes, spinach | **+ Cooked proteins** (avoid raw egg whites, which block biotin) | Raw egg whites contain avidin, which blocks biotin |
| **B9** | Folic acid, folate | Red blood cells, DNA, cell division, prevents birth defects in pregnancy | Leafy green vegetables, legumes, citrus fruits, asparagus, fortified cereals, avocado | **+ B12**: working together they form red blood cells | Alcohol and heat destroy folate |
| **B12** | Cyanocobalamin | Red blood cells, nervous system, energy, memory, cognitive health | Meat, fish, eggs, dairy, fortified cereals | **+ Folic acid (B9)**: essential combination for blood and nerves | Antacids reduce absorption |
| **C** | Ascorbic acid | Antioxidant, collagen (bones/vessels/skin/teeth), immune system, iron absorption, wound healing | Citrus fruits, strawberries, peppers, broccoli, tomatoes, kiwi, Brussels sprouts | **+ Iron** (meat, spinach, lentils): **increases absorption 2–4x** when eaten together | Coffee/tea block iron (do not take with iron-rich meals) |
| **D** | Cholecalciferol (D3), ergocalciferol (D2) | Absorption of calcium/phosphorus, strong bones/teeth, muscles, blood calcium | Fatty fish, fortified milk, fortified cereal, egg yolks, **sun** | **+ Fats**: fat-soluble, absorbs better with avocado/oil/nuts | Avoid taking it without fat in the meal |
| **E** | Tocopherol | Antioxidant, protects cells, membranes, red blood cells, immune system | Nuts, seeds, spinach, broccoli, vegetable oils, avocado | **+ Fats**: fat-soluble, absorbs better with oil/avocado/nuts | Heat/light oxidation destroys vitamin E |
| **K** | Phylloquinone, menaquinone | Blood clotting (prevents bleeding), healthy bones | Leafy green vegetables, broccoli, cabbage, soybean oil, chicken, cheese | **+ Fats**: fat-soluble, needs oil/avocado/cheese for absorption | Antibiotics may reduce intestinal production of K2 |

## Star Combination: Vitamin C and Iron
🍊🥩

### Why eat them together instead of one after the other?

| Situation | Effect on iron absorption |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| **Orange + meat together** ✅ | Vitamin C combines with iron in the stomach, forming an easily absorbable compound |
| Orange first or meat first ⚠️ | Less effective—the iron and vitamin C need to be **present together during digestion** |

### Key points:

- Meat contains **heme iron** (from animal sources), which the body absorbs well (15–35%).
- Vitamin C **increases iron absorption 2–4 times** when consumed together.
- Even for heme iron from meat, vitamin C provides an **additional boost**.

### Best approach:

- Eat **orange slices with your meat** or drink **orange juice during the meal**.
- You can add orange juice to a salad with meat, or have **fruit for dessert right after** while it is still in the stomach.
- **Avoid coffee, tea, or dairy** with the meal — they block iron absorption!

## Other Fruits and Foods Rich in Vitamin C
🍓🫐

| Food | Combine with | Benefit |
|------|--------------|---------|
| **Red peppers** | Spinach, lentils, beans, chicken | **3x more vitamin C than oranges** per serving |
| **Strawberries** | Oatmeal, nuts, yogurt | Increases iron absorption from plant foods |
| **Kiwis** | Chicken, fish, salads | **2x the vitamin C of oranges**; excellent with plant iron |
| **Cantaloupe** | Ham, eggs, grilled chicken | Vitamin A + C combination |
| **Broccoli** | Fish, eggs, tofu | Vitamin C + calcium synergy |
| **Lemons/limes** | Fish, leafy greens, beans | **Squeeze over salad or fish** for an instant boost! |
| **Pineapple** | Pork, chicken, salads | Vitamin C + enzymes that help digestion |

## Other Powerful Combinations for Maximum Health
🥑🍅

| Combination | Why it works |
|-------------|--------------|
| **Leafy green vegetables + olive oil or avocado** | Helps absorb **fat-soluble vitamins A, D, E, K** |
| **Tomatoes + olive oil** | Increases lycopene absorption up to **4x** |
| **Carrots + fat** (butter/oil/nuts) | Increases vitamin A absorption |
| **Eggs + avocado/kimchi** | May increase antioxidant absorption **9x** |

🍽️

- **Oatmeal** + strawberries or dried apricots
- **Baked chicken** + roasted grapefruit for dessert
- **Meat + citrus sauce** on top
- **Kale salad** + tuna + green peas

## What to Avoid with Iron-Rich Meals
☕🥛

| Food/drink | Negative effect |
|------------|-----------------|
| **Coffee/tea** ☕ | Tannins **block iron absorption** |
| **Dairy/calcium supplements** 🥛 | **Compete with iron** for absorption |

---

## Key Conclusion
✅

- **Eat colorful fruits and vegetables at every meal**
- **Add healthy fats** to salads, leafy greens, and tomatoes
- **Combine plant iron** (beans, spinach) **with vitamin C** for optimal absorption
- **Eat foods together, not one after the other**, for maximum nutritional synergy
